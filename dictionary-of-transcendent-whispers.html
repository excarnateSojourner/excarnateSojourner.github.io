samasterre

Etymology

From Old English sama + Middle English sterre.

Inspired by someone else's post in the Dictionary of Obscure Sorrows subreddit (which has now been made private, so I can't find / link to the post). They provided the definition, and using Wiktionary I constructed the word and etymology.

Pronunciation

IPA: /sæməˈstɛɹ/

Definition

Noun: Wondering at the fact that every human who has ever gazed at the night sky has looked at the exact same Moon and stars as you.

existential wonder

Etymology

existential + wonder

Definition

Noun: When you imagine a small piece of media, especially one that is not meant to be taken seriously, as having incredibly deep lore and meaning. As if a comedic YouTube video or song is the only surviving evidence in our universe of an equally complex parallel universe. When Couchville becomes Narnia.
